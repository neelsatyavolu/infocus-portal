# InFocus Social Media Design

How every InFocus Instagram post and story looks. Logo, colors and type come from the main `DESIGN.md` in this folder; this page covers what's specific to social.

Make posts in the Portal: **Managers → Social media → Instagram Post Maker**.

## The short version

- Warm white background, near-black text, InFocus Green for small labels.
- Lexend for words, Geist Mono for numbers and times.
- Square corners, flat color. No gradients, shadows, glows or emoji.
- Every slide ends with a footer: the source (or infocusnews.tv) and **@infocusnews**.
- One logo per slide.

## Sizes

| | Size | Margins |
|---|---|---|
| **Post** (default) | 1080 × 1350 | 80 px sides and top, 72 px bottom |
| **Story** | 1080 × 1920 | 80 px sides; keep text out of the top and bottom 250 px (Instagram's buttons cover them) |

## Layout

Every slide, top to bottom:

1. **Header:** section label (kicker) on the left, the wordmark on the right, a thin rule under both.
2. **Headline.**
3. **Date or byline** in Geist Mono, right under the headline.
4. **Body:** a photo, points, a number, a chart, a quote.
5. **Note** (optional): one or two sentences of context.
6. **Footer:** a thin line, then the source or infocusnews.tv on the left and @infocusnews on the right.

If it doesn't fit, cut words or add a slide. Don't shrink the margins.

## Color

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Warm White | `#FAF7F0` | Background |
| Panel | `#F1ECE1` | A highlighted row or box |
| Line | `#DDD6C8` | Dividers, empty part of bars |
| Ink | `#0F110F` | Headlines and main text |
| Secondary | `#3B3933` | Dates, notes, body text |
| Muted | `#6E695F` | Source line, small print |
| InFocus Green | `#0B6E3E` | Kicker, @infocusnews, bullets, one accent |

Colors that carry meaning are used only for that meaning:

| | Hex |
|---|---|
| Democrat | `#0059C2` |
| Republican | `#CD332E` (`#B8281F` for text) |
| Independent / other | `#6B726D` |
| Yes | `#1A8F55` |
| No | `#8A918C` |

Record Red (`#EE3A2A`) stays in the logo. Never use party colors for decoration.

## Type

| Element | Size | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Kicker | 28 px | Lexend SemiBold, ALL CAPS, +18% spacing, Green |
| Headline | 112 px (84 px beside a photo) | Lexend SemiBold, −2% spacing |
| Date / byline | 30 px | Geist Mono, Secondary |
| Body | 40 px | Lexend Regular, Secondary |
| Label | 26–28 px | Lexend SemiBold, ALL CAPS, +11% spacing |
| Note | 28 px | Lexend Regular, Secondary |
| Footer | 24 px | Lexend Regular, Muted; handle SemiBold Green |

Two weights per slide. No italics, no underlines, no ALL CAPS names. Keep small print at 24 px or larger.

## Photos

- Real photos only: our own, or one we have permission to use. Credit them under the photo.
- No text straight on a photo. Put it below the photo or on a plate.
- Use the same kind of photo for everyone in a story (all portraits, or all action shots).

## Writing

- Sentence case. Headlines about 12 words or fewer.
- Plain verbs. No hype ("insane," "huge"), no clickbait.
- Facts are checked and sourced. Anything with numbers gets a source and a date.
- Results: say "leads" until the race is called, then say who called it.
- AP style for dates and times: `Nov. 17, 2026`, `7:13 PM PT`.

## Dark version

Ink background (`#0F110F`), Soft White text, the white wordmark. It matches the on-air graphics; use it for a post that should feel like the broadcast. Don't mix white and dark slides in one carousel.

## Before you post

- [ ] Names, numbers and dates checked.
- [ ] Source and date on anything with numbers.
- [ ] Photo credited.
- [ ] Nothing in a story's top or bottom 250 px.
- [ ] Someone else has read it.
