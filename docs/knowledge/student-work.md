# Student cycle work

Sidebar **The Cycle** (students, and APs who are members of a package):

| Tab | Route | What to turn in |
|---|---|---|
| Information | `/information` | How the cycle and approval work |
| Brainstorming | `/brainstorming` | Three proof-of-contact images + brainstorm Google Doc |
| A-roll/B-roll | `/a-roll` | Footage uploads to Drive |
| Initial Cut | `/initial-cut` | Polished draft; revisions until stage 1–3 pass |
| Final Cut | `/final-cut` | After stage 3; the video plus a one-line headline (e.g. "Palo Alto Airport Day brings the community together") and a toss for the anchors; every EP scores each member on quality /25 and effort /25 |

Tabs unlock in order. Later tabs are visible but disabled ("This stage is locked until the previous one is approved"):

- A-roll/B-roll opens when the producer approves Brainstorming (proof of contact).
- Initial Cut opens when the producer approves A-roll/B-roll.
- Final Cut opens when Stage 3 approves the Initial Cut.

**Brainstorming**: upload three proof-of-contact images (one per slot) and paste the brainstorm link, then **Save link**. The link must be a Google Docs or Google Drive `https://` link. Any group member can replace or remove a proof. When all three proofs and the doc are in, the assigned producer gets an email and push. Each proof upload also posts to the class Slack proof-of-contact channel: one post per group with the members' names, topic, cycle, and the proofs so far, replacing the group's earlier post.

**A-roll/B-roll**: pick A-roll or B-roll, then one or more clips (up to 30 GB per A-roll file, 15 GB per B-roll file). The assigned producer gets an email and push the first time footage is uploaded each day. Once the producer approves A-roll/B-roll, uploads close; new producer feedback reopens it.

**Initial Cut**: each upload is a new version of the same cut (**Upload new version**). The version goes to whichever stage is reviewing it, and that stage's reviewers get an email and push. No more uploads once Stage 3 approves.

**Final Cut**: one upload. A second upload is allowed only as the second revision after a low first grade (see grading.md).

**Feedback**: producers post feedback on each stage; students read it in the stage's feedback panel but cannot post there. **View Feedback** and the sidebar tab show a count of unread feedback. Members get an email and push for new feedback, and when a producer approves Pitching, Brainstorming, or A-roll/B-roll (with the producer's note, if any). Initial Cut decisions (Stage 1 approved, Stage 2 approved, sent back for revisions, approved for Final Cut) also email and push the members.

Uploads go to InFocus Drive under Package Storage / Cycle N / {group name} / {stage}. Camera originals (Sony XAVC, ProRes) play in the browser via Drive's H.264 proxy, not the raw file.

**Drag and drop**: instead of clicking Upload, you can drag files from your computer onto the upload box. A-roll/B-roll takes one or more video clips, then asks whether they are A-roll or B-roll. Initial Cut takes one video and uploads it right away. Final Cut takes one video, then asks for the headline and toss (below) before uploading. On Brainstorming, drop one image onto an empty proof-of-contact slot (one proof uploads at a time). Files of the wrong type are skipped with a message. Dropping a file outside the box, or while an upload is running, does nothing, so the page doesn't leave.

Producer review of that work is on Groups, except the student tabs are where students upload and read feedback.

Pitching has no student upload; the producer mark on Groups is the check-in signal.

**Final Cut headline**: clicking **Upload** on Final Cut first asks for the package headline (required, up to 100 characters, no `<` or `>`), then opens the file picker. The headline becomes the Final Cut video's title. It shows at the top of Final Cut for students and producers on Groups, on the Publishing Queue card, and as the YouTube title. A second-revision upload asks again, prefilled with the current headline. Final Cuts uploaded before headlines were required have none, so their YouTube title stays the package topic.

**Final Cut toss**: the same Upload dialog also requires a toss (up to 500 characters, no `<` `>` `[` `]` `{` `}`, since those are teleprompter cues): the lines an anchor reads right before the package airs, e.g. "Last month, hundreds of community members gathered at the Palo Alto Airport to celebrate its 25th anniversary. InFocus reporters Abby and Otto went there to learn more." It shows under the headline on Final Cut. Group members (and producers who can act on the package) can add or edit it any time after upload with **Add a toss** / **Edit**, including Final Cuts uploaded before tosses were required. The toss fills in the show's teleprompter script (see teleprompter.md).

**Approved for Final Cut**: when Stage 3 approves the Initial Cut, members get an email and push reminding them to upload the Final Cut (with the cycle's Final Cut due date when one is set; groups with an approved extension get no date) and linking straight to `/final-cut`. The Initial Cut page also shows an **Upload Final Cut** banner until the Final Cut is uploaded.

**Package of the Cycle**: when a group's package wins, its Final Cut page shows a banner with **Download certificate** (a printable PNG with the member's full name, the package headline, and the cycle).
