# Student cycle work

Sidebar **My package** (students, and APs who are members of a package) opens the stage you are working on. It shows your unread feedback count and, only when there is something to act on, a second line with the stage and one tag: **Revisions**, **Extension** (an extension request needs your answer), or **Due today** / **Due tomorrow** / **Due Thu** when that stage closes within 72 hours and nothing is turned in yet (Pacific time, counting approved extension days). A stage bar at the top of each page switches between stages and shows each stage's status and unread feedback count; at its end are **Cycle guide** (the Information page) and, for students, **Extension requests** (more in navigation.md):

| Tab | Route | What to turn in |
|---|---|---|
| Cycle guide | `/information` | How the cycle and approval work |
| Brainstorming | `/brainstorming` | Three proof-of-contact images + brainstorm Google Doc |
| A-roll/B-roll | `/a-roll` | Footage uploads to Drive |
| Initial Cut | `/initial-cut` | Polished draft; revisions until stage 1–3 pass |
| Final Cut | `/final-cut` | After stage 3; the video plus a one-line headline (e.g. "Palo Alto Airport Day brings the community together") and a toss for the anchors; every EP scores each member on quality /25 and effort /25 |

These tabs show the current cycle (the first one whose Final Cut date has not passed). Exception: if the student's package in the previous cycle has no Final Cut yet (no upload and not marked done by a producer), the tabs stay on that previous cycle until the Final Cut is in. If the student is also on a group in the new cycle, the cycle card at the foot of the sidebar shows a switch (for example "Cycle 2 | Cycle 3") so they can open either cycle and submit to it (or ask the assistant to switch); the pick is remembered in that browser. Producers still see the new cycle on Groups.

Stages unlock in order. Later stages show in the stage bar but are disabled (hovering says "Unlocks when the previous stage is approved"); opening a locked stage's page says "This stage is locked." and which approval unlocks it:

- A-roll/B-roll opens when the producer approves Brainstorming (proof of contact).
- Initial Cut opens when the producer approves A-roll/B-roll.
- Final Cut opens when Stage 3 approves the Initial Cut.

**Brainstorming**: the **Materials** card holds three proof-of-contact images (one per slot) and the brainstorm Google Doc link. Upload the proofs, paste the link, then **Save link** (or press Enter). The link must be a Google Docs or Google Drive `https://` link. A tag beside the field says **Linked**, **Not linked** or **Unsaved changes**, and emptying a saved link turns the button into **Remove link**. Any group member can replace or remove a proof. When all three proofs and the doc are in, the assigned producer gets an email and push. Each proof upload also posts to the class Slack proof-of-contact channel: one post per group with the members' names, topic, cycle, and the proofs so far, replacing the group's earlier post. If a producer posts feedback without approving, the stage shows REVISIONS until the producer approves or a member uploads or replaces a proof. **Where it stands** says what is still missing or what happens next, with a link on to A-roll/B-roll once Brainstorming is approved. To see another cycle, pick it from the cycle menu in the header (shown when there is more than one cycle); the page address then keeps `?cycle=N` (left off for the current cycle). Someone on two packages in one cycle switches between them with tabs under the page header, each showing its status and unread count.

**A-roll/B-roll**: pick A-roll or B-roll, then one or more clips (up to 30 GB per A-roll file, 15 GB per B-roll file). The assigned producer gets an email and push the first time footage is uploaded each day. Once the producer approves A-roll/B-roll, uploads close; new producer feedback reopens it.

**Initial Cut**: each upload is a new version of the same cut (the button reads **Upload Initial Cut** the first time, then **Upload new version**). The version goes to whichever stage is reviewing it, and that stage's reviewers get an email and push. No more uploads once Stage 3 approves.

**Final Cut**: one upload. A second upload is allowed only as the second revision after a low first grade (see grading.md).

**Feedback**: producers post feedback on each stage; students read it in the stage's **Feedback** panel, which is always on the page (beside the work on wide screens; on narrower screens in the same column, moved to the bottom while it has no notes, and showing the latest three notes with **Show all**), but cannot post there. Notes you haven't read carry a **New** tag (the panel title shows how many, e.g. **2 new**), and the panel marks the stage read once it is on screen. Each note has **Mark resolved** for when you've handled it: it gets a **Resolved** tag and dims, and **Reopen** undoes it. Group members and the package's producer can resolve notes; it doesn't change the stage's status or grades. The stage bar (per stage) and **My package** in the sidebar (all stages) show a count of unread feedback. Unread notes left on A-roll/B-roll clips show as **N new on clips**; open a clip to read them. Members get an email and push for new feedback, and when a producer approves Pitching, Brainstorming, or A-roll/B-roll (with the producer's note, if any). Initial Cut decisions (Stage 1 approved, Stage 2 approved, sent back for revisions, approved for Final Cut) also email and push the members.

Uploads go to InFocus Drive under Package Storage / Cycle N / {group name} / {stage}. Camera originals (Sony XAVC, ProRes) play in the browser via Drive's H.264 proxy, not the raw file.

**Drag and drop**: instead of clicking Upload, you can drag files from your computer onto the videos panel (**Footage**, **Versions** or **Your Final Cut**); it outlines while you drag. A-roll/B-roll takes one or more video clips, then asks whether they are A-roll or B-roll. Initial Cut takes one video and uploads it right away. Final Cut takes one video, then asks for the headline and toss (below) before uploading. On Brainstorming, drop one image onto an empty proof-of-contact slot (one proof uploads at a time). Files of the wrong type are skipped with a message. Dropping a file outside the panel, or while an upload is running, does nothing, so the page doesn't leave.

Producer review of that work is on Groups, except the student tabs are where students upload and read feedback.

Pitching has no student upload; the producer mark on Groups is the check-in signal.

**Page layout** (A-roll/B-roll, Initial Cut, Final Cut): the header names the cycle and stage with the stage's status tag, the package topic, and the group and its producer, with **Refresh** (it also checks for new feedback) and the one Upload button. Beside the videos on wide screens (first on narrower ones), **Where it stands** says what to do next, shows the Initial Cut approval path (Stage 1 producer review, Stage 2 adviser review, Stage 3 executive sign-off, each with its state), and repeats the stage's upload rule. The newest Initial Cut version plays on the page (its status is the stage's, in the header); each earlier version shows its own review status. The Final Cut video carries its review status as a tag (**Pending review**, **Approved** or **Needs revisions**, set on its review page), separate from the stage's Submitted / Approved / Queued status.

**Final Cut headline**: clicking **Upload Final Cut** (**Upload revised Final Cut** on a second revision) first asks for the package headline (required, up to 100 characters, no `<` or `>`), then opens the file picker. The headline becomes the Final Cut video's title. It shows on Final Cut (as the video's title and in the **What airs** card) for students and for producers on Groups, on the Publishing Queue card, and as the YouTube title. A second-revision upload asks again, prefilled with the current headline. Final Cuts uploaded before headlines were required have none, so their YouTube title stays the package topic.

**Final Cut toss**: the same Upload dialog also requires a toss (up to 500 characters, no `<` `>` `[` `]` `{` `}`, since those are teleprompter cues): the lines an anchor reads right before the package airs, e.g. "Last month, hundreds of community members gathered at the Palo Alto Airport to celebrate its 25th anniversary. InFocus reporters Abby and Otto went there to learn more." It shows under the headline on Final Cut. Group members (and producers who can act on the package) can add or edit it any time after upload with **Add a toss** / **Edit toss** in the **What airs** card (a **No toss** tag shows while it is missing), including Final Cuts uploaded before tosses were required. The toss fills in the show's teleprompter script (see teleprompter.md).

**Approved for Final Cut**: when Stage 3 approves the Initial Cut, members get an email and push reminding them to upload the Final Cut (with the cycle's Final Cut due date when one is set; groups with an approved extension get no date) and linking straight to `/final-cut`. Until the Final Cut is uploaded, **Where it stands** on the Initial Cut page says so and its main button is **Go to Final Cut**.

**Package of the Cycle**: when a group's package wins, its Final Cut page shows a banner with **Download certificate** (a printable PNG with the member's full name, the package headline, and the cycle).
