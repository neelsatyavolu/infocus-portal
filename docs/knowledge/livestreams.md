# Livestreams

Route: `/livestreams`. Everyone opens it from **Livestream Tracker** in the sidebar, under Production (producers see it below **The Show**). For producers and livestream managers, that sidebar item shows how many sign-up requests are waiting.

Semester 1 hours accumulate immediately, but livestream grades stay **—** and are excluded from totals until **November 30 at midnight Pacific**. Grade Editor and student grade views still show **x/8 hours completed** during that time. In any semester, the grade also stays **—** until at least one livestream in that semester is marked **Completed**, so an empty tracker is never a silent 0%.

The page is titled **Livestreams**; its eyebrow says **Production** and the semester. Producers and appointed livestream managers see three tabs under the page header: **Schedule**, **Sign ups** (with the number of requests waiting), and **Completion**. Switching tabs also refreshes the schedule, and the page remembers your tab in this browser. Everyone else sees only the Schedule, and requests slots right on it. The header has **Refresh** (↻), **Live dashboard**, **Managers** (producers), and, on the Schedule tab, **Add event** (producers and livestream managers; the **N** key opens it from any tab). On phones, managers find Refresh, Managers and Live dashboard under **⋯**.

Above the schedule is a status strip of four numbers. Students see **Your hours** (completed hours out of 8, with a bar), **Next call time** (the next livestream you're crewing, with the arrival time), **Open slots** (open crew seats on every Scheduled livestream), and **Requests** (your pending requests, and how many were denied). Producers and livestream managers see **Next livestream** (its day and title), **To mark completed** (past livestreams still marked Scheduled), **Open slots**, and their own progress (**Your hours** for producers, **You managed** x / 4 for appointed managers). On phones the strip shows the first two; the other numbers lead the line next to the schedule filters. When something changed for you since your last visit (added to a crew, a request denied, hours credited), a **Since your last visit** note lists it (green when it is all good news) and those livestreams get a small green dot; new sign-up requests get the same dot on Sign ups.

Filters above the schedule: **All**, **Open** (upcoming livestreams you can still request) and **Mine** (livestreams you crew or asked to crew) for anyone who can sign up, and **Needs action** (requests to review or past livestreams still marked Scheduled) for producers and livestream managers. Each filter except All shows its count, and the choice is remembered in this browser. The line next to them counts the semester's livestreams: total, scheduled, completed and cancelled.

The schedule lists **Today and upcoming** livestreams first. Livestreams from earlier days are under **Earlier this semester**, which folds away (open by default for producers and livestream managers, closed for everyone else, and remembered in this browser). Past livestreams still waiting to be marked Completed come first, and for producers and livestream managers the group's header shows how many there are. When a filter is on, both groups are simply listed. On a wide screen the schedule is a table; on narrower screens and phones each livestream is a card. Each livestream shows when, the title, location, availability (**Public**, **Unlisted**, or **Unconfirmed**), manager, crew, hours, and status. **When** shows the crew **arrival time** and, under it, the day and the **event start time**. Events saved before arrival times existed show **Arrival TBD** until a producer or livestream manager edits them. The crew shows attendees by first name with a count against capacity, and Scheduled livestreams carry a tag: **Full**, or the number of open seats (amber for producers and livestream managers when the livestream is within a week). **Hours** shows your own credited hours (marked *yours*) when they differ from the event's. Status tags: **Completed**, **Cancelled**, and for producers and livestream managers **Not completed** on a past livestream still marked Scheduled; a Scheduled livestream has no status tag. Your own status shows as **On crew**, **Pending**, or **Denied**. In the table, on a past livestream where a producer could both sign up and mark it complete, **Complete…** stays on the row and **Sign up** (or **Request again**) and **Edit** are under its **⋯**.

Full credit: **8 completed hours per semester** (5 pts/hour → 40 pts in the Packages category). Hours come from attendees on **COMPLETED** events in the semester window (S1 Aug 13–Dec 18, S2 Jan 5–Jun 3). Appointed livestream managers instead earn **10 pts per COMPLETED livestream they manage** in the semester (the event's Manager field), so **4 managed livestreams = 40 pts**; extra managed events are not extra credit, and zero-hour (credit-cancelled) events do not count.

The **Completion** tab hides execs only: EPs, the adviser, and the super admin. Associate producers still need livestream credit and stay listed. Manager rows show a **Manager** tag and **x / 4 managed** instead of hours. Search by name or email, or pick **Below full credit** to see who still needs livestreams; the line beside them counts how many are at full credit, and the page header states the credit rule.

Everyone can view the schedule. All signed-in members, including producers, can request sign-up unless they are appointed livestream managers. Appointed livestream managers cannot request sign-up, even if they are also producers. Producers and appointed livestream managers create events, mark complete, and manage attendees.

## Sign ups

To ask for a slot, press **Sign up** on a livestream in the Schedule (the **Open** filter lists them), add an optional note, and press **Request sign-up**. The livestream then shows **Pending**. You can't request a livestream that is full, cancelled, already completed, or one you're already crewing, and you can have only one pending request per event. After a denial, **Request again** appears. Producers who can sign up do it the same way. Appointed livestream managers can't request slots; they assign crew while editing an event.

Producers and livestream managers review requests on the **Sign ups** tab: one card per livestream, with its crew count and open seats (amber when the livestream is within a week), and each request with the person, **Partial availability** when it applies, their note, and how long ago they asked. **Approve** adds the person to the crew (it fails if the event is full or cancelled); **Deny** turns it down. On the Schedule, **+N requests** on a livestream jumps to its card on Sign ups. Completion updates once the event is marked Completed.

## Events and managers

Producers and livestream managers use **Add event** and **Edit** (the pencil on each livestream, or click the row or card): event name, date and event start time, crew arrival time (same day), location, status (Scheduled, Completed, Cancelled), availability, default credit hours, capacity (default 4, up to 50), **Event manager**, crew, and notes. Both times are required to save. Default hours and each person's credit hours must be from 0 to 24 in quarter hours, and capacity a whole number from 1 to 50; a value out of range is flagged beside its field. The event start time is what the Livestream dashboard thumbnail and **Starting soon** countdown, the Class Board, and the public upcoming list use; the arrival time is only for the crew. Add crew by searching members by nickname, name, or email and picking a person (Enter adds the top match); the crew is a table with one row per person, their own credit hours, and a remove button. Hours are required to mark an event Completed. On a past livestream still marked Scheduled, **Complete…** opens the editor with the status set to Completed and the cursor in the hours. **Delete** removes the event. **⌘↵** / **Ctrl+Enter** saves from any field. Any member can be picked as Event manager (appointed livestream managers are listed first), but only appointed livestream managers earn manager credit for it.

Producers (associate and up) appoint and remove livestream managers with **Managers** at the top of `/livestreams` (under **⋯** on phones). Managers can edit the schedule, approve sign-ups, and view completion; they are not producers.

## Adjust credit

Producers and appointed livestream managers can open **Edit** on a livestream and change **Default credit hours** for the crew. In the crew table’s **Credit hours** column, enter a person’s hours (0–24), use **0** to remove their credit, or leave blank to use the event default. Individual overrides are absolute hours, not percentages.

**Cancel credit** sets the event default to **0**, awarding no credit to anyone even if individual overrides exist. Save to apply. The event and attendance remain recorded. Restore a positive default to award credit again; saved individual overrides then apply again. Only **Completed** events award credit; Scheduled and Cancelled events award none. Adjustments feed completion totals and official grade calculations.

## Livestream dashboard

Route: `/live`. Anyone signed in to the Portal can open it, from **Live dashboard** at the top of `/livestreams`. Signed-out crew can open `/live` with the 6-digit **livestream dashboard PIN**; only producers and appointed livestream managers can see it, in **Settings → PINs → Livestream dashboard PIN** (**Create PIN** the first time, **New PIN** to replace it). It is separate from the Class Board PIN. A PIN session lasts until midnight Pacific and only opens the dashboard. Replacing the PIN ends every PIN session. The PIN locks after 20 wrong tries in 15 minutes. On the PIN screen, **Esc** clears what you typed. The dashboard works on phones: its tabs sit at the bottom of the screen, and each tab's main buttons sit in a bar just above them (**Start clock** / **Stop clock** with the clock and score beside it, or a save problem when there is one; **Clear** and **Push to OBS** with what's on air; **Download PNG**). Volleyball has no clock, so its **End set** stays in the Set card. Both bars step aside while you type in a field.

The header shows the open livestream's title, day, start time (Pacific), and location, and, when you're signed in, **Back to Livestreams**. To open another one, use **Switch livestream** in the header (it lists livestreams from 12 hours ago through the next 45 days; cancelled events are left out). Older ones are under **Past livestreams** at the bottom of the same list (the 40 most recent, newest first), for example to make a Final score thumbnail the next day. The dashboard opens on the soonest current livestream; a past one is only opened when you pick it. Then use one of three tabs; the dashboard starts on Scoreboard and remembers your tab while the browser tab stays open. Several people can run the same livestream at once (one on the clock, one on the score) without undoing each other.

- **Thumbnail**: template **A · Matchup** (games), **C · Event** (everything else), or **Final score** (after a game), in **YouTube** 1280 × 720, **Instagram post** 1080 × 1350, or **Instagram story** 1080 × 1920. Fields fill in from the event title, start time, and location. A title like "Varsity Boys Basketball vs. Gunn" (also v., versus, or @) starts on Matchup with Home **Paly**, Away **Gunn**, and Level and sport **Varsity Boys Basketball**; team names are up to 18 characters. Pick the template and fill in the fields in the **Thumbnail** card; pick the format at the top of the **Preview** card. **Download PNG** (at the bottom of the Thumbnail card, with the image size) saves the image. Instagram versions add “Live on YouTube · @infocusnews”; stories keep the top and bottom 250px clear. **Final score** is for after the game: it says **Final**, shows each team's name and score with the losing score dimmed, and leaves out the start time. **Home score** and **Away score** fill in from the scoreboard when you open the tab (sets won for volleyball, so tap **End set** on the last set first) and can be typed over, up to 999. Download the YouTube size and set it as the video's thumbnail in YouTube Studio; the Portal doesn't upload it. Its Instagram versions say “Replay on YouTube · @infocusnews”.
- **Scoreboard**: drives OBS source 1. Sports: **Basketball** (bar with a green tier for Bonus and timeouts), **Football** (clock in the middle, down and distance above the team with the ball, three timeout pips per team, restored at halftime), **Volleyball** (a row per team with sets won, serve dot, and highlighted points; **End set** records the set), and **Other** (free-text period, a clock that counts up or down with a set period length, and a note on the strip). Each team has a name and a color; names can be up to 20 characters, and long names shrink on the bug to fit. Score buttons: basketball +1 / +2 / +3, football +6 / +3 / +2 / +1, volleyball +1, Other +1 / +2 / +3, plus **−1** for mistakes; scores roll to the new number on the bug. Each team card shows **Ball**, **Bonus**, **Serving**, and **Timeout** tags, and football's timeouts left. Basketball quarters start at 8:00 (overtime 4:00) and show tenths under a minute; football quarters start at 12:00. The next-quarter arrow resets the clock and clears basketball's Bonus. **Show on stream** (at the top of the clock card) hides or shows the clock; **Reset clock** puts it back. Football: **Give ball** sets 1st & 10 for that team; on the **Down and distance** card, pick a down, a quick distance (10, Goal, Inches) or type one in **To go**, or **Hide down**. Tap a score or the clock to type a new value (for example 4:32 or 45.5); **−10s / −1s / +1s / +10s** nudge the clock, the arrows beside the quarter step back or forward, and **Space** starts or stops the clock when you're not typing. **Board setup** holds **Sport**, **Position** (the bug at the bottom, the default, or top), and **Reset scoreboard**. **Call timeout** shows a tag for 8 seconds; tap **End timeout** to take it down sooner (football also takes a timeout pip, and ending early doesn't take another; **+1 timeout** gives one back). **Flag** turns football's down tab yellow (and shows a **Flag** tag) until you tap it again, set a new down, or someone scores (it clears itself after 2 minutes). In football, **+6** plays a green TOUCHDOWN plate with the team name over the bug for about 5 seconds; **−1** right after cancels it, and **Touchdown animation** on the Down and distance card turns it off. The volleyball bar always shows the current set; a finished set's result (for example SET 1 · PALY 25–20) shows beside it for 10 seconds. Scoring clears football's down. Switching sport or **Reset scoreboard** clears the score back to 0–0 but keeps team names and colors (both ask first). The **On stream** card shows the bug as viewers see it, what it's showing now (a timeout, flag, touchdown, or set result), and the OBS link.
- **Live image**: drives OBS source 2. Pick a graphic from the strip at the top to queue it, fill in its fields on the **Queued** card, then **Push to OBS** (at the top of the Queued card). Only you see the queue; viewers see nothing until you push. **Not pushed** on the Queued card means you changed the fields of the graphic that's on air but haven't pushed them yet. **Clear**, next to Push to OBS, sets the source back to transparent. The **On air** card shows what viewers see and since when. Graphics: **Starting soon** (countdown to the event's start time; **Start offset (minutes)** moves the target, such as 60 when the stream starts an hour after the listed time or -10 to start ten minutes early, and blank means no offset), **Commentators** (two lower thirds), **Player spotlight** (name and stat line), **Halftime** (score and period totals), **Be right back**, and **Final score** (final score, then the Follow InFocus handles). Halftime and Final score read the score from the scoreboard.

Tags beside **Livestream dashboard** in the header show what's going on:
- **Past** when a past livestream is open. Changes still save to it.
- **On air** with the graphic's name while a live image is on air, on every tab.
- The scoreboard's save state: **Saving** (only when a save takes a moment), **Saved**, **Retrying** while a failed save is tried again, and **Not saved** when a change was turned down.
- **Not syncing** when changes from other devices haven't arrived for about 8 seconds.

While you're on another tab, the **Live image** tab shows a red dot when a graphic is on air, and the **Scoreboard** tab shows a dot while a save is retrying or was turned down, or changes aren't syncing.

When a scoreboard save fails, the reason shows in full on the clock card (the Set card for volleyball; on the other tabs, at the top of the page), with when it retries. The OBS bug keeps the last saved score until then. If the livestreams or the open livestream can't load, **Try again** loads them again.

Each livestream has two OBS Browser Source links, both 1920 × 1080 with a transparent background: `/live/<key>/scoreboard` and `/live/<key>/image`. They are read-only and need no sign-in, so the key is the secret. Producers and livestream managers can press **Make new OBS links** (under each OBS link) to replace the key; the old links stop working. Overlays check for changes every second.
