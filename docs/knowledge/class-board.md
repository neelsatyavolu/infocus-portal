# Class Board

Route: `/class-board`.

Everyone signed in can open `/class-board` directly. It is not in the sidebar. It is the classroom display for the current package cycle.

Super admins and the adviser can open **Settings** and use **Class Board PIN**. The PIN is six digits. Entering it on `/class-board` opens that page only. It does not sign anyone into Portal. **New PIN** replaces the old one, and devices using the old PIN have to enter the new one. Twenty wrong tries in 15 minutes pause further tries.

Packages are lanes in a race, ordered by how many stages are cleared. At the same stage, work that needs revisions ranks ahead of work pending review, and both rank ahead of work not yet submitted. For example, Version 2 Needs Revisions ranks above Version 2 Pending Review. Among Initial Cut revision requests at the same stage, higher versions rank first: Version 2 Needs Revisions ranks above Version 1 Needs Revisions. Packages at the same stage and step share a place, with revision requests tied only when their versions also match. Seven dots match the workflow milestones: pitch, contact, A-roll/B-roll, Initial 1, Initial 2, Initial 3, final cut. The three Initial Cut milestones follow the approval chain rather than upload versions or check-in credit. When the current cut needs revisions, the board keeps its dot at the stage that requested those revisions: adviser feedback stays at Initial 2. Feedback from older uploads does not move the current cut forward, and withdrawn approvals still move the dots back. For every cycle, Initial 1 shows the regular Initial Cut deadline. Initial 3 is three calendar days before Final Cut, and Initial 2 is four calendar days before Initial 3 (seven days before Final Cut). These dates also appear in Next deadlines. Stages 2 and 3 are display-only reminders and do not change grades or the graded Initial Cut deadline. Without a Final Cut date, those two reminders stay unset. A filled dot is cleared. The ringed dot is the stage in progress. The line is green through cleared stages. The note on the right is the same status shown on a Groups tile, including pending review, revisions, and the approval stage. Empty roster rows are hidden.

Livestreams lists scheduled events for the next 14 days, with crew, open slots, and public / unlisted / unconfirmed. Master Calendar lists the weekdays in that same window: show, PA, class, or no school, plus anchors, PA announcers, show manager, and any packages on the publishing queue that day.

The page fills the available browser viewport, including taller classroom displays. Package lanes stretch through the full board height, with titles and statuses allowed up to two lines. The right column uses one third of the available width and divides its full height between Livestreams, Next deadlines, and Master Calendar, keeping all three on screen together. The sidebar and assistant are hidden here. The wordmark sits to the left of the Class Board title and returns to Dashboard or Packages. The browser fully reloads the Class Board page automatically every 30 seconds, refreshing the leaderboard and all other board content. On the production URL, this also picks up UI and code changes after a new deployment goes live. The clock continues updating every second.

During **Period 1**, the existing header also shows **Class focus** and a live minutes-and-seconds countdown. The focus suggests the next action for the nearest upcoming deadline across cycles, including the Initial Cut review stages; it shows the cycle and whether the deadline is today, tomorrow, or further away. If no deadlines remain, it suggests planning the next package steps. The countdown turns amber for the last ten minutes. The focus and countdown are centered in the existing header, between the left-aligned title and right-aligned clock, and disappear at the end of class.

Period 1 times come from [Paly’s public bell schedule](https://www.paly.net/campus-life/daily-bell-schedule), using the subscription feed linked from that page. The board checks the feed hourly and follows its recurring periods, holiday exclusions, special schedules, moved class days, and finals in Pacific time. Master Calendar no-school overrides also hide the strip. If the feed is unavailable, the schedule is unclear, or the day has no Period 1, the board stays usable with the strip hidden.

When mirroring a Mac to the classroom TV, open the board in a full-screen browser. If black bars surround the entire mirrored desktop, choose the TV as the display to optimize for in macOS **System Settings → Displays**. The board can fill the browser viewport, but cannot change the mirrored display's resolution or remove bars outside that viewport. See [Apple's TV display instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp1206/mac).

Below Livestreams, **Next deadlines** lists the soonest stage dates still ahead, across cycles. The contact and filming stages are labeled **Proof of Contact & Brainstorming** and **A-roll & b-roll**. Each deadline is a row directly within the panel, separated by a subtle divider, with an amber stage dot, stage name, calendar date, cycle number, and a Package Cycles-style countdown pill such as in 8d. Rows share the panel background instead of using nested cards. Cycle 1 Initial Cut Stage 2 is September 22, 2026, and Stage 3 is September 26, 2026. Future cycles automatically use the same seven-day and three-day offsets from their Final Cut deadline; changing Final Cut updates the reminders. Grading still uses the regular Initial Cut deadline. Dates already passed are left off. A deadline today says Today. The next seven are shown.
