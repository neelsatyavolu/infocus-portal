# Settings, notifications, onboarding

## Onboarding (`/onboarding`)

First sign-in sends you here before any other page. It is laid out like Settings: **Profile** → **Nickname** (required, up to 60 characters; the name Portal shows, Google keeps your account name), then **Notifications** with an **Email** card (on/off and an optional **Send to** address) and a **Browser** card (on/off), each with its own **Send me** switches for Announcements, Comments, and Grades. A channel's Send me switches can be changed only while it is on. **View as** skips onboarding. The browser switch here only saves your choice; turn on **Browser** in Settings → Notifications on each device you want notified. While **Finish onboarding** can't be pressed, the reason shows beside it (no nickname yet, or Email on with no address to send to). If your saved choices fail to load, an error shows with **Try again** (until you change something), and you can still finish with the choices on screen.

## Settings (`/settings`)

Sections are listed down the left on wide screens (a strip across the top on phones), in the order below. Most changes save at once; only the nickname and the notification address have a **Save** button.

The list shows a tag beside a section when something needs a look. **Notifications** shows one once your settings load: **On**, **Off** (every channel is off), **Blocked**, **Not set up** or **Not registered** (this browser or the InFocus app can't show them yet), or **Error**. **PINs** and **Signature** show **Not set** or **Error**, and **Profile** shows **Error**, only when that applies. On phones, where the strip can hide a tag, the page header lists the warnings and errors as links to their sections.

- **Notifications**: one card per channel, each with its own **Send me** list (Announcements, Comments, Grades).
  - **Email**: on/off, **Send to** (optional; blank uses your account email) with **Save**, the Send me switches, and **Send test** (only while Email is on). New accounts start with email on and Grades off.
  - **Browser** (on the web): on/off, the Send me switches, and **Send test**. Turning it on asks the browser for permission and registers that device; do it on each device you want notified. The switch is for your whole account: turning it off stops browser notifications on every device. The test only shows a notification on this device. **Blocked** means this browser blocks Portal's notifications (allow them in its site settings); **Unsupported** means it can't show them.
  - **Mac app notifications** / **iPhone app notifications** (only inside the InFocus Mac app or the InFocus Portal iPhone app, in place of the Browser card): status, **Turn on** (asks macOS or iOS; shown until you answer), **Open Mac/iPhone settings**, and **Send test** (sent through Apple to every Mac and iPhone you're signed in on). It has no switches of its own: its Send me list shows the Email settings it follows; see below.
- **Appearance & playback** (saved in that browser): **Theme**, System (the default: follows the device's light or dark setting), Light or Dark, also shared across the Portal subdomains (grades, teleprompter, equipment); the teleprompter run mode and video players stay dark. **Default view** (Grid or List), **Autoplay videos on open**, and **Start with sound on**. From October 15 through October 31 five more switches appear: **Halloween** (the Halloween package's pumpkin-and-purple colors and logos, spooky background music, cobwebs in the corners, and visitors: pumpkins to click and smash, candy to catch, bats, a ghost, a spider, a black cat, a witch on a broom, glowing eyes and a skeleton hand; on by default; the switch shows how many pumpkins this browser has smashed and how much candy it has caught), **Spooky sounds**, **Spooky music** and **Surprise BOOs** (something jumps out with a loud "BOO!": a ghost, a jack-o'-lantern, a spider, eyes in the dark or a bat swarm; the first within seconds of opening the Portal, then every 60–90 seconds; never during a video or teleprompter run mode) and **Hauntings** (every 25–45 seconds something strange happens to the page for a few seconds: the lights flicker or go out except for a flashlight on the pointer, blood drips, handprints, a cracked screen, static, a glitch, a shadow walking past, a heading that briefly reads "I SEE YOU", fog, and the like, or a sound such as footsteps, a howl, thunder or a distant scream; everything stays clickable, and nothing happens during a video, a dialog, typing or teleprompter run mode; the tab title calls you back while you're on another tab). The music starts after your first click on a page, pauses while a video plays, the teleprompter is in run mode or the tab is in the background, and also has a quick mute button just left of the ✱ button. Typed outside a text box, b-o-o sends out a burst of ghosts, t-r-e-a-t showers candy, t-r-i-c-k shakes the page, s-c-a-r-e sets off a BOO and h-a-u-n-t sets off a haunting. The first time a browser opens the Portal in the season, a Halloween welcome explains all this, hints at the hidden tricks and points here to turn things off; it has no off switch of its own and shows once per browser. Clicking the strutting turkey makes it hop; five clicks on one turkey pardons it (it gets a gold medal and walks free). ## Mac and iPhone notifications

The InFocus Mac app and the InFocus Portal iPhone app get a notification for **every email Portal sends you**, at the same moment, with the same rules: if your email settings would skip an email (for example Grades off), there's no Mac notification either. The title is the email's subject (or a shorter one for package events), and clicking it opens the email's link in the app. Exceptions, email only: sign-in codes, account invites, access-request decisions, and the Settings test email. Turn the whole app on or off in macOS System Settings → Notifications → InFocus, or on iPhone in Settings → Notifications → InFocus Portal. A Mac or iPhone stops getting notifications when you sign out of the app. App notifications follow your account email: mail Portal sends to a different **Send to** address has no app notification.

## What Portal emails and pushes

Every email here except sign-in codes, invites, and access decisions also reaches the InFocus Mac app (see **Mac and iPhone notifications** above). Only announcements, grades, and the legacy video-uploaded email follow your email switches; the other emails below are sent to whoever needs to act, whatever those switches say.

## Review reminders

An hourly job nags whoever is holding work up, 12 hours after it started waiting and again every 12 hours until they act:

- **Initial Cut**: the assigned associate producer for Stage 1, the adviser for Stage 2, and every executive producer and super admin who hasn't approved yet for Stage 3. The clock starts at the latest upload or the previous stage's approval. Packages sent back for revisions (waiting on the students) are skipped.
- **A-roll/B-roll**: the assigned producer, while footage waits for approval (it scores 0/5 until approved). The clock starts at the latest upload; producer feedback after that upload means the students owe the next move, so it stops.
- **Final Cut grading**: each Final Cut grader (executive producers and super admin) who hasn't scored every member yet, counted from the latest Final Cut upload.
- **Participation docks**: the adviser, the other executives, and super admin (not whoever submitted them), while docked scores wait for approval. Email only.

Email and push, except participation (email only). Initial Cut and A-roll cover the active and later cycles; Final Cut grading also covers the cycle before the active one.

## Deadline reminders

At 7:30 AM Pacific, every group member whose pitch, proof of contact, A-roll/B-roll, Initial Cut, or Final Cut is due tomorrow gets an email and push if the group hasn't turned it in yet. Each member's own approved extension days move their deadline, so a member with an extension gets it the day before their own deadline instead. Turned in means: the pitch marked by a producer, all three proof-of-contact images and the brainstorm doc, any A-roll/B-roll upload (approval is the producer's job), or an Initial or Final Cut upload. It goes out once per group, stage, and due date, whatever the Email switches say.

## Slack

- **Announcements** on `/announcements` are read from the class Slack announcements channel.
- **Proof of contact**: each proof upload posts to the class proof-of-contact Slack channel as the Portal bot (members, topic, cycle, and the proof images), replacing that package's earlier post.
- Opening Portal refreshes Slack announcements and reposts any missing proof-of-contact posts (at most once a minute).
- **Class Board standings**: 7 PM Pacific Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, the Class Board standings as an image in the class board channel.
- **Birthdays**: 12:00 AM Pacific, a happy birthday with an animated card in #announcements for everyone born that day (see Settings → Profile above).
- **Thanksgiving leaderboard**: Nov 1–5, every three hours except 3 AM Pacific, the leaderboard's top five in #announcements until the board goes dark at 11 PM Thursday; then 8 AM Friday Nov 6, the winner with the final top five.

## Scheduled jobs you might notice

- **Hourly**: review reminders (above) and a database backup (Admin → System → Backups, kept 7 days). From 7:30 AM to 8:30 PM Pacific, the show manager's role-confirm reminder (every 3 hours until confirmed).
- **Every minute**: meeting reminders, 15 and 5 minutes before the start (see `meetings.md`). Meeting calendar changes reach Google Calendar in the background.
- **Every 10 minutes**: alerts for the public InFocus iPhone app (new show, new story, a stream going live; see `iphone-apps.md`).
- **Every 15 minutes**: Portal checks for queued packages and shows due to upload to YouTube on their air date. While an upload is in progress it checks every minute until it finishes. Starting a show upload begins right away.
- **Daily**: deadline reminders (7:30 AM Pacific); anchor and PA reminders (9 PM and 8 AM Pacific); the unscheduled show-edit reminder (6 PM Pacific); Slack birthdays (midnight Pacific); the next 21 days of InFocus Producer Meetings are added to Meetings; equipment overdue emails (morning, Pacific); any Master Calendar edits not yet copied to the Google Doc are synced; media left in trash for 7 days is deleted for good; older versions of a video are removed once its current version is a week old.
- **Weekly**: livestream hours reminders, Fridays 8 AM Pacific, only in the two weeks before livestream grades post. Slack Class Board standings Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at 7 PM Pacific.
- **Every 2 days**: a new set of story ideas on the Ideas tab, written in the afternoon (4:30pm Pacific in daylight time, 3:30pm in standard time). Nobody is notified; see `ideas.md`.
