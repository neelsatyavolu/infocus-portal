# Members

Route: `/members` (sidebar Producing → **People**). Producers only: associate producers, executive producers, the adviser, and super admin. Students are sent to Access denied. Participation moved to the Grade Editor's **Participation** tab (executives only).

A list of class members (not EP, adviser, or super admin) with **general** notes and **per-cycle** notes. This is not the account list and not the package roster.

- The cycle menu in the page header picks the notes you are working on: **General** (notes that stay with the person all year) or one cycle (**Cycle 01 · focus**, …).
- **Person** and **Class** (beside the cycle menu) switch the view.
- **Person** shows the list of members on the left and one member on the right: the chosen note, their **General** note (when a cycle is chosen), and a short preview of each other cycle's note (**Open** switches the page to that cycle). Cycles with no note are listed in one line. The arrow buttons beside the name step to the previous or next member. On a phone, tap a name to open it, and tap **Members** (back arrow) or use your phone's Back to return to the list.
- **Class** shows every listed member's note for the chosen cycle on one page, to read or write the whole class's notes in one pass.
- At the top of the list: search by name or email (`/`), sort by last name, first name, or email, and **All**, **With note**, or **No note** for the chosen cycle (with counts). In Person view, the small squares on each row show which notes that person has (General, then each cycle; the chosen one is outlined).
- Type in a note. It saves on its own a moment after you stop typing, when you click away, when you switch member, cycle, or view, or when you leave the page. Up to 4,000 characters per note (a count shows near the limit). If a save fails, the note shows **Couldn't save** and the message at the top of the page has **Try again**.
- Keys: in Person view, `J` / `K` next or previous member, Enter on a focused name puts the cursor in their note, and ⌘Enter or Ctrl+Enter saves and moves to the next member. Esc leaves a note in either view.
- The page remembers your view, cycle, sort, filter, and selected member in this browser.
- Notes are shared: every producer who can open Members reads and edits the same note. Students never see them.

To let someone sign in, use **Add person** at the top of Admin Dashboard (**Overview** or **People** tab). To put them on a package, use Package Cycle.
