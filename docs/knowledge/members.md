# Members

Route: `/members`. Producers only: associate producers, executive producers, the adviser, and super admin. Students are sent to Access denied.

A list of class members (not EP, adviser, or super admin) with **general** notes and **per-cycle** notes. This is not the account list and not the package roster.

- Bottom tabs: **General** (notes that stay with the person all year) and one tab per cycle (**Cycle 01**, **Cycle 02**, …).
- Type in a member's box. It saves on its own a moment after you stop typing, when you click away, or when you switch tabs. Up to 4,000 characters per note.
- Notes are shared: every producer who can open Members reads and edits the same note. Students never see them.
- Search by name or email. Sort by last name, first name, or email.

To let someone sign in, use Admin → People. To put them on a package, use Package Cycle.
