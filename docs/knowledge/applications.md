# Applications (leadership applications)

`applications.infocuspaly.com` (also `applications.infocusportal.com`). It isn't in the sidebar: open it from a direct link. It uses your normal Portal sign-in.

## Students

- The home page lists open applications (this year: the Executive Producer application; anyone not selected as an EP is automatically considered for Associate Producer). A round shows up once executive producers publish it and its open time has passed.
- The application has three sections plus a review step: **Quick facts** (grade next year, full years in InFocus, current and previous InFocus roles, leadership experience, weekly hours on other commitments, other commitments, skills (check any, and add up to 5 of your own, 50 characters each), and whether you are enrolling in InFocus next year: answer Yes only if you know for sure), **Short answers** (two questions with a word limit and live word count), and **Rough-cut feedback** (watch a rough cut, write notes in the box under the notes list the way Review comments work: each note is saved at the video's current moment with a category, typing pauses the video, notes stay in time order and the time jumps the video there; Edit and Delete show on hover. Then a top-three summary and an air decision with one sentence of reasoning).
- Answers save automatically as you type ("Saved" at the top). You can leave and come back.
- **Review & submit** shows everything, lists anything still missing, and needs the attestation checkbox before you can submit.
- Deadlines are enforced by the server in Pacific time. After the deadline you can't submit unless an executive producer gave you an extension.
- Before the deadline you can **Unsubmit to edit** (if the round allows it), then submit again.
- After executive producers release decisions, your own decision and its message appear on the home page and on your application. You never see other applicants, scores, rankings, notes or votes.

## Executive producers, super admin and adviser

The **Review** tab (top bar) lists rounds. Executive producers and the super admin can create and edit rounds, rank, vote and release; the adviser can see everything but doesn't vote or change settings.

- **New round** creates a hidden draft from this year's template. **Settings** edits the window (visibility, open and due times, unsubmit), every option list, the question wording and word limits, the rough-cut video (paste the Portal link of an uploaded video), its context and the reviewer-only **answer key**, the AI rubric, and the **decisions** (labels, colors and the message each applicant sees).
- **Applicants** has every applicant in a list on the left (grade, submit date, how many execs picked them, AI standing, votes; filter by status or "No vote from me") and the selected application on the right. On a phone the list and the application show one at a time. **CSV** downloads the comparison table.
- **Add mock applicants** adds made-up applicants (tagged Mock) so you can try everything; **Remove mocks** deletes them and any exec test copies. A reviewer who opens the student form gets a test copy tagged Mock.
- Each application shows the answers beside the rough cut (click a note's timestamp to jump there), **Interview prep** questions, private **Reviewer notes**, and **Interview transcripts** (paste the recorded interview's transcript; each shows who added it and when, folded closed with a word count), the **AI read**, the final-decision vote, and for EPs an extension and an optional personal decision message. **Interview sheet** is a printable page per applicant.
- **AI read** (reference only) scores the written answers 1–5 on each rubric criterion and shows where each applicant stands relative to the others ("3rd of 9"). It never sees names, grades or class records. Rough-cut scores compare the notes with the answer key.
- **Decision desk**: each executive picks 2 applicants for Executive Producer and 3 for Associate Producer (counts and lists are in Settings; a person can only be in one list), with a reason for each, then submits. Other executives' picks stay hidden until you submit yours. The board shows how many execs picked each applicant for each list, who picked them and why, the AI standing and the vote.
- **Final vote**: each executive producer and the super admin picks a decision per applicant. A decision needs a majority of all voters; otherwise it shows Split.
- **Release**: schedule a time (Pacific) or release now, once applications have closed (after the deadline, or with the round set to Closed) and no extension is still running. Release needs a majority decision for every real applicant, freezes those decisions, locks votes and locks the form for applicants. Cancel or un-release to change votes.
- **Settings** refuses a save made from an out-of-date page (someone else saved, or another tab); reload and make the change again.
