# Grading

Weighted **55 / 35 / 10**: Packages / Participation / Portfolio.

Every cycle deadline closes at **11:59 PM Pacific** on its date (check-ins release, Final Cut lateness, and when a cycle stops being current). Exception: the 2026-27 cycle 1 Final Cut closed at 2 AM Pacific on Sep 30.

Null = ungraded (a dash, excluded). `0` = graded and earned nothing. A day-one student has no grade, not 0% F. The Portal assistant can clear a cycle grade back to ungraded; that is not the same as setting 0.

## Packages (55%)

Per cycle: 50 pts final cut + 20 pts check-ins + 40 pts livestream (5 × 8 hours).

Semester 1 livestream grades release **November 30 at midnight Pacific**. Before then, livestream scores show **—** and contribute neither earned nor possible points to grades or the Grade Editor Total/CSV defaults. Completed hours remain visible as **x/8 hours completed** in the Grade Editor and student grade views. On release, completed hours count at 5 points/hour, capped at 40 (appointed livestream managers instead get 10 points per managed completed livestream, capped at 4 = 40); an empty tracker remains ungraded. Semester 2 timing is unchanged.

**Check-ins** (4 × 5 = 20 per cycle; S1 reporters 60, S2 80; associates 40 / 60): pitching, proof of contact, a-roll/b-roll, initial cut. After that stage's deadline, PoC and Initial Cut earn credit from **submission**. PoC counts as submitted once all three proof-of-contact images and a Google Doc brainstorm link are in. **A-roll/B-roll requires producer approval**; uploaded work awaiting review or needing revisions earns 0/5 until approved. Pitching has no upload: being on the package is the turn-in. An approved extension moves every check-in deadline for the students it covers by the granted days (an October 14 Initial Cut deadline with a 10-day extension becomes October 24), so nothing is docked before that date. Grade Editor counts only check-ins whose deadline has passed (earned out of 5 per due stage), the same as the student gradebook; stages not due yet count toward neither number. Final cut is not a check-in.

**Final cut grade:** graded per member on the Groups **Final Cut** tab (`/groups`). Every executive producer and the super admin scores each member on quality (out of 25) and effort (out of 25), 50 total. The adviser and associates do not grade. A member's score counts only after every one of those graders has scored them; it is the average of their totals, to a tenth. After the effective deadline, missing approved final cut is 0/50. After the first graded cut, if any member is below 75% the group may upload one second revision (otherwise a Final Cut cannot be replaced); those members are re-scored and capped at 37/50, and members at 75% or above keep their grade. Uploading that replacement Final Cut wipes every executive's quality and effort scores on the package, so graders score the re-scored members again; their earlier grade stays until all graders finish. Late: 20% after the member's extension deadline, 30% more than 14 days late; a second revision does not remove the 30%. Lateness is measured from the Final Cut upload time, or the **Turned in** date in Grade Editor. Graders enter their scores under **Member grades** and press **Save my scores** (on phones that bar stays on screen while scores are unsaved). Each member row there shows the **Late penalty** that member gets. Executive producers and the super admin can change it to 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, or 30%, or set it back to **Automatic**. The change applies at once: an already graded member's official score is recomputed. Other producers see the penalty but can't change it.

**Associate quota:** associates need 2 packages in semester 1 and 3 in semester 2. A cycle they are not on counts for nothing, not 0. Only their best required cycles count toward the grade, so an extra package can replace a weaker one. If they can no longer reach the quota once the remaining Final Cut deadlines pass, the missing cycles count as 0. Every cycle counts for regular reporters.

## Publishing cycle grades

Publish on the Groups Final Cut tab (**Publish grades** at the bottom of the **Cycle grade** section, with one shared cycle note up to 2,000 characters; it is the filled button once every member has an official grade) or in Grade Editor (**Publish** on a row, or **Publish all** in the page header for every row with a Final Cut score; its count says how many). Until then, the student's cycle row on `/grades` says **In progress** and hides the producer note and the **View package feedback** link. Publishing does not change the score: a member's Final Cut points already count in their grade, and show on that row, as soon as every grader has scored them. Newly published students are notified per their Settings: **Publish grades** on Groups sends email only; Grade Editor **Publish** sends email and browser push.

Changing a published grade in Grade Editor unpublishes it and marks it **Revised**; publish it again and the student sees **Published · revised**. Once a row has been published or revised, the history button after **Publish** / **Unpublish** opens that row's publish history.

Student view: `/grades` (also `grades.infocusportal.com`) has two tabs at the top. **Overview** (key `1`) shows the estimated grade with the Packages, Participation and Other (livestream + portfolio) totals; **Packages**, one row per cycle this semester (status, Final cut, Check-ins, the four check-in stages, the producer note and **View package feedback**); **Livestream & portfolio**; and **Participation**, one week at a time (**Previous week** / **Next week**, or the ← / → keys). Anything posted, published or revised since your last visit on that browser (cycle scores, a graded week, livestream points, the portfolio) gets a **New** tag, and the header says what changed; changed weeks read "(new)" in the week list, and **View** under it opens the latest one. **Gradebook** (key `2`, `?tab=all`) is the All Grades table with what-if (`-` ungraded, `\` exempt; Enter / Shift+Enter move between score boxes; nothing is saved). Old `?tab=packages`, `participation` and `other` links open Overview at that section. The **Grades** sidebar link shows a dot when a grade has been published since your last visit to `/grades`.

Producer view: `/grade-editor`, with view tabs across the top: **Cycle 1**, **Cycle 2**, …, **Total grade**, and **Student**. With the keyboard, ← / → move between the tabs and Enter opens one. Once the missing report has loaded, a cycle tab shows an amber count of people missing or unpublished there. The page opens on the view you used last in that browser; on a first visit it opens on Total grade, then moves to the latest cycle whose Final Cut date has passed (Pacific time). Each view has one panel: **Search reporters** (`/` focuses it; it only narrows what is shown), a **First name** / **Last name** sort, **Refresh**, and **Download CSV** (every reporter in the current sort, whatever the search or filter). On phones the sort and these actions are in the ⋯ menu. On phones and tablets each reporter is a row that opens a sheet with the same fields, feedback and Publish, plus **Previous** / **Next** and **Open gradebook**. On a computer the reporter column stays pinned on the left and, on cycle tabs, **Status** and **Actions** stay pinned on the right, so only the middle columns scroll sideways when the window is narrow. Cycle tabs group the columns as **Final cut** (right after the reporter), **Check-ins**, **Semester**, and **Publishing**. Check-ins are **Pitch**, **PoC** (proof of contact + brainstorming), **A/B-roll**, and **Initial** (hover a header for the full name), plus a read-only **Total** out of 20 for that cycle with its percentage (released stages only; ungraded and exempt stages are left out). Each dropdown shows N/A until its deadline (or when an associate is not participating in that cycle); otherwise it reads **5 · auto** or **0 · auto** (the automatic score) until overridden, with a green border once a manual score is set. Partial credit shows amber and 0 shows red. Executives and the adviser can select a score from 0–5, **Ungraded**, or **Exempt**; it saves immediately and changes the actual check-in grade, student gradebook, and totals. Choose **Auto** to clear an override and return to the stage-based score. These edits do not change stage approval or publish/grade the Final Cut. The **Semester** group shows the same **Participation** earned/possible (and percentage) as the Total grade tab. In **Publishing**, **Status** shows one tag per row: **Published**, **Revised** (changed after publishing and not published again), **Unpublished**, **Not entered**, **Missed**, **Exempt**, or **Ungraded**; a grade that was revised and then published again reads **Published** and says so on hover. **Actions** holds the speech-bubble feedback button (green when feedback exists), **Publish** / **Unpublish**, and the publish history button.

A missed Final Cut shows as **0** in Grade Editor, the same as the student gradebook: once the member's effective deadline has passed with no approved Final Cut and no score entered, the cycle tab tags the row **Missed** and shows 0.0% in red beside the Score box, and the Total grade tab shows a red 0 for that cycle and counts it as 0/50. The Score box stays empty (placeholder 0) and nothing is saved or published until a producer types a score. **Ungraded** and **Exempt** still exclude it, and an associate is not zeroed for a cycle they skipped.

In Grade Editor cycle tabs, **Ungraded** means no grade has been assigned; **Exempt** means the work is excused. Both remove that item's earned and possible points from totals, including after its deadline. Zero remains a graded zero. Check-in **Automatic** is separate: it restores deadline-based scoring.

**Final Cut** scores in Grade Editor cycle tabs are typed text fields, not a dropdown (the dropdown is only for check-in columns). To mark a final cut:
- **Exempt:** type a backslash `\` in the Final Cut field.
- **Ungraded:** type `-` or clear the field.
- **Scored:** type a number from 0 to 50 (rounded to a whole number). This replaces the averaged score.

For a late member the Score box shows the score after the late penalty; clicking into it shows the score as entered, which is what you edit. Each cycle tab also shows the score as a percentage of 50 beside the Score box, after any late penalty: a late member's percentage has an amber **Late** mark, and hovering it shows the score before and after the penalty (phones show the score with "late"). An unsaved edit shows the typed score until it saves. The cycle average and the Total grade tab's cycle columns also use the score after the late penalty, as the student gradebook does. Each cycle tab also has **Feedback** for each member (up to 2,000 characters), and **Turned in** (locked, with a lock on its header and a notice linking to **Cycle Dates**, until the cycle has a Final Cut date). In a Score box, Enter moves to the next row and Shift+Enter to the previous one. The header line gives the Final Cut date, how many grades are published, the cycle average, and how many people are over their extensions (those rows are tagged **Over ext.**); the save tag beside **Grade Editor** reads **All saved**, **N unsaved**, or **Saving** (rows save on their own as you type, and **Save all** in the panel saves the rest now). While **Save all** or **Publish all** runs, the other cycle tabs and **Open cycle** wait until it finishes. Filter chips with live counts narrow the table: **All**, **Unpublished**, **Not entered**, **Missed**, **Revised**, and **Over extensions**. A chip keeps the rows it matched when you clicked it, so a row doesn't vanish while you edit it; click it again to update. The chosen chip is remembered in the browser.

**Missing grades** (a header button, with a count) opens a side panel that lists, cycle by cycle, for every cycle that has at least one grade, people with no grade (**Not entered**) or an unpublished one (**Unpublished**). Exempt final cuts do not appear. **Open cycle N** goes to that cycle, filtered to the people still to enter or publish. **Exclude** on a row, or the **Exclude people** tab (with a search), hides people from this list and its counts in your browser only.

The **Total grade** tab's editable score fields accept the same `-` and `\` shortcuts, including Check-Ins, Livestream, Final (the portfolio), and Extra. These are temporary calculation/CSV adjustments, not saved official grade edits. Extra credit counts in the Packages share of the weighted total. CSV exports distinguish ungraded and exempt values and exclude their possible points. **Notes** saves a note per reporter on its own as you type (up to 5,000 characters). In the **Student** tab's what-if fields, `-` and `\` simulate ungraded/exempt; clearing a field restores the official score.

Producer view: `/grade-editor` to enter scores; the Student tab is one reporter's All Grades table (what-if does not save). Super admin / adviser can also ask the Portal assistant to look up a grade or propose a quality / portfolio change; that still needs **Approve** on the chat card.

## Participation (35%)

50 pts/week: Monday PA 10, Tuesday class 20, Thursday class 20. Show days (Wed/Fri) none. Holidays 0. Finals weeks 0 (every day, Monday to Friday; see Calendar and show). Mondays with no class (no Monday schedule that week, e.g. Sep 28, 2026) are 0: the list is `NO_MONDAY_CLASS_2026_27` in `src/lib/school-schedule.ts`. First gradeable day: Tuesday, August 18, 2026.

The Grade Editor **Total grade** tab groups its columns as **Total grade** (second, right after the reporter and pinned beside it: a letter tag beside the weighted percentage, the same 55 / 35 / 10 weighting as the student gradebook over work due or graded so far), **Packages** (one column per cycle plus a read-only **Subtotal**), and **Semester credits** (Check-ins, Livestream, Participation, Final, Extra). Notes open from the speech-bubble button at the end of the row. A reporter with temporary edits is tagged **Edited**. It shows Participation as **earned/possible (X/Y)** for the current semester. Both numbers include only graded days in weeks that have fully ended: Sunday through 11:59:59 p.m. Pacific stays excluded, then the week is included at Monday midnight. Current/future weeks, ungraded days, holidays, and nonparticipation days contribute to neither number. Before any eligible grades exist, the column shows 0/0. These points count in the Total grade tab's Participation share (35%) and the CSV export. Edit participation through `/participation`.

Associate producers and above enter scores on `/participation` (People → **Participation** tab), one week at a time: the **Week** view, with **Previous week** / **Next week** and **This week** (keys `[` and `]`). On a computer it is a table with one column per class day; days off (Wed/Fri shows, holidays) only get a column when someone has a score or comment there. On a phone or tablet, pick the day in the card header and enter each student's score in a list. **Full** gives everyone full marks for that day; **Full week** on a row fills that student's week. The comment button on a cell adds a note (it saves only with a score). **Save** sits in a bar at the bottom of the page (⌘S or Ctrl+S), which counts unsaved changes; switching weeks with unsaved changes asks first. Full marks post immediately. A dock below that day's max needs a **second** producer to approve: any associate, executive, adviser, or super admin other than the one who entered it, in the **Pending** view (the count on **Pending**, beside **Week**, is every request waiting, in amber when there are any; the People link and the Participation tab count only the requests you can approve; **Approve** / **Deny** on each request). Executives, the adviser, and super admin are emailed when a request is waiting. Pending docks stay off the student gradebook.

## Portfolio (10%)

Final portfolio out of 100. Grade Editor has no portfolio column to save it; the super admin or adviser sets it through the Portal assistant (with **Approve**). The Total grade tab shows it as **Final**.

## Who is on the roster

Grade Editor and Participation omit executive producers, super admin, and the adviser.
